# 🎂 Prajakta's Birthday Celebration Website 💖
> *A high-quality, interactive, romantic birthday experience dedicated to **Prajakta** (affectionately known as **Praju** and **Madam Ji**).*

---

## 🌟 How to Open & View the Website

1. Simply double-click **`index.html`** in this folder (`C:\Users\ASUS\Desktop\Birthday\index.html`).
2. It will open instantly in your default web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Brave, Safari, etc.).
3. No web server or internet connection is required — everything works completely offline!

---

## 💖 Features Included

1. **Surprise Unboxing Gate**:
   - A 3D animated gift box with starlight particles.
   - Click to unwrap: bursts with colorful confetti, plays a celebration chime fanfare, and starts the gentle romantic birthday melody.

2. **Interactive 3-Tier Birthday Cake**:
   - Realistic glowing candles with flickering flames.
   - Click candles or click **"💨 Blow Out Candles"** to blow them out with a realistic smoke effect and sound!
   - Virtual **"🔪 Cut the Cake"** button: animates a fresh slice of cake for Madam Ji with confetti and celebration fanfare!
   - **"🔥 Relight Candles"** option so she can make wishes again and again.

3. **"Why Madam Ji is Irresistibly Special" (Crush Compliments)**:
   - 6 elegantly styled cards highlighting her radiant smile, charm, elegance, kindness, adorable laugh, and magnetic personality.

4. **Polaroid Memory Wall (Photo Gallery)**:
   - Retro polaroid frames with washi tape and handwritten notes.
   - **Easy Photo Uploading**: Click the camera icon `📷` on any card or click `➕ Upload Real Photo of Prajakta` to drop in real photos of Prajakta right from your computer!
   - Click on any polaroid photo to open the fullscreen **Lightbox zoom view**.

5. **A Secret Note for Madam Ji (Envelope with Wax Seal)**:
   - A vintage envelope with a wax seal stamped **"M"** (for Madam Ji).
   - Clicking the seal smoothly slides open an unfolding heartfelt letter expressing genuine admiration, warmth, and birthday wishes.

6. **Madam Ji's VIP Scratch Card**:
   - Interactive scratch card: use your mouse cursor or finger to scratch off the silver layer.
   - Reveals sweet birthday treats and crush privileges (e.g. *Unlimited coffee treats*, *100% Always Right pass*, *Genie wish pass*).
   - Includes a **"New Scratch Card"** button with multiple random treats.

7. **Pop the Birthday Balloons Mini-Game**:
   - Floating colorful balloons drifting upwards across the screen.
   - Click or tap any balloon to pop it with a crisp sound effect and unlock personalized sweet wishes.

8. **Birthday Wish Wall**:
   - Interactive message board with sticky notes.
   - Type your name and a sweet message, click **"Pin Wish"**, and watch a pastel sticky note appear with a pushpin!

9. **Music & Audio Controls**:
   - Built-in Web Audio API romantic melody synthesizer (plays soothing music box tones).
   - **Custom Song Loader**: Click `Music` -> `📁 Custom Song` to load your own MP3 or audio track directly from your computer!
   - Sound effects toggle (🔊/🔇) and theme switcher (🌸 Rose Gold / 🌙 Starlight Midnight).

## 🎨 How to Customize

- **Add Real Photos**: Place image files in this folder or simply use the photo upload button on the website.
- **Change the Secret Letter**: Open `index.html`, find the `<div class="letter-content">` section, and edit any text to add your own personal memories or jokes.
- **Hosting Online**: You can upload this entire folder to **GitHub Pages**, **Netlify**, or **Vercel** to get a free public link you can share with Prajakta over WhatsApp or Instagram!
